# Access and document policy — fictional demonstration data

New employees request application access through the service desk. Their manager approves the business need, and the platform team grants the least privileged role. Production access expires after thirty days unless renewed.

Team documents are searchable by readers and administrators. Administrator-only documents are visible only to administrators. Readers cannot upload or delete documents. An administrator must review document classification before upload.

Rotate service credentials after suspected exposure. Revoke the old credential, provision the replacement through the secret manager, update the service configuration, and verify the application before closing the security ticket.
